Physics

Explores the fundamental laws of nature. Students engage in theoretical learning and hands-on laboratory experiments to comprehend physical phenomena and transition smoothly to BS Physics.

Apply Now Explore Programme
Duration2 Years (4 Terms)
Credit Hours69 Credits
SessionSpring & Fall
Degree Code10220701

Program Overview

Explores the fundamental laws of nature. Students engage in theoretical learning and hands-on laboratory experiments to comprehend physical phenomena and transition smoothly to BS Physics.

Electromagnetism

Understand the forces that power our modern technological world.

Quantum Mechanics

Explore the fascinating behavior of matter at the atomic level.

Lab Mastery

Extensive laboratory sessions to prove theoretical concepts.

Curriculum Structure

The ADP is structured over four semesters with a careful blend of General Education Courses (GEC) and major-specific modules.

Mechanics 3 CR
Physics Lab I 1 CR
Calculus I 3 CR
Functional English 3 CR

Electricity and Magnetism 3 CR
Physics Lab II 1 CR
Calculus II 3 CR
Islamic Studies 2 CR

Waves and Oscillations 3 CR
Modern Physics 3 CR

Thermodynamics 3 CR
Quantum Mechanics 3 CR
Physics Lab IV 1 CR
Differential Equations 3 CR

Admission & Fees

Eligibility Criteria

Minimum 45% marks in intermediate (FSc/ICS) or equivalent.

Tuition Fee

Total estimated fee: Rs 334,300 (Standard ADP rate).

Teaching & Assessment

Teaching Methodology

Lectures, tutorials, extensive laboratory sessions, and theoretical problem-solving.

Assessment Methods

Written exams, laboratory practical exams, quizzes, and project reports.

Career Pathways

Lab Technicians, Research Assistants, Quality Control Analysts, Educators, and Scientific Support Staff.

Further education and certification, or stepping directly into the 5th semester of a BS program, can significantly enhance career prospects and open doors to global opportunities.

Lab Technician

Operate and maintain complex scientific equipment.

Research Assistant

Support groundbreaking research in physics and engineering.

QC Analyst

Ensure manufacturing quality using physical testing methods.

Science Educator

Teach foundational physics in academic institutions.
