Business Administration PhD

The PhD Business Administration program at the University of Chenab is vast and multifaceted.

Program Overview

The PhD Business Administration program at the University of Chenab is vast and multifaceted. It encompasses in-depth research and advanced study in business administration such as finance and management. Doctoral candidates delve into complex theoretical frameworks, empirical analyses, and interdisciplinary approaches to address pressing issues in the business world. This scope extends to exploring topics like organizational behavior, leadership, sustainability, and corporate social responsibility, etc. Graduates of such programs are expected to contribute significantly to the academic field and provide valuable insights to inform business practices, policy-making, and strategic decision-making in an ever-changing global economy.

Offers

From a social and employment perspective, the PhD Business Administration program at the University of Chenab offers a broad scope. It equips graduates with advanced research, analytical, and leadership skills, making them highly sought-after in academia, industry, and government. They can pursue careers as educationists, researchers, consultants, or top-level executives, driving innovation and strategic development in organizations. Additionally, their expertise in business theory and practice can influence policymaking, fostering economic growth and sustainability. This social impact extends to mentoring future business leaders and contributing to the knowledge base of business, ultimately benefiting society by improving business practices and facilitating informed decision-making in an increasingly complex and interconnected global business environment.

Assessment

All the modules are assessed by coursework, including individual assignments, real-business case studies, interview projects, online quizzes, group work, and poster presentations.

Duration

The minimum duration for completion of PhD degree is Three years. As allowed by HEC, it can be completed in a maximum period of Eight years.

Entry Requirement

Requirments

 
Prior to entry into a PhD program, the student shall have been awarded MS/M.Phil. or equivalent degrees as described in this policy.

  1. For admission in PhD programs, a minimum CGPA of 3.0 (out of 4.0 in the semester system) or First Division (in the annual system) in the most recent degree obtained is required, whether such degree was obtained from Pakistani or foreign universities.
  2. In case of foreign degree, if the CGPA/Grade is not mentioned on the transcript, the candidate must produce equivalent weightage from the parent university.
  3. The student having strong demonstrated interest in obtaining PhD degree but has done previous degree without thesis/research/dissertation, then he/she must complete 6 credit hours deficiency thesis or courses or thesis + courses to be eligible for PhD program (as decided by admission committee). The deficiency courses/thesis will be non-credit.
  4. To gain admission, students must achieve an aggregate score of 70% on the university-specific admission test and interview. However, if a candidate submits a GAT Subject (NTS) score, they will be exempted from the university’s admission test and will only need to appear for the interview.
  5. The University reserve the rights to cancel any admission due to any violation/noncompliance to rules and regulations set in policy documents.
