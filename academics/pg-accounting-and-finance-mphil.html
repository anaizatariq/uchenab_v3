Program Overview

About Program

The Master of Philosophy in Management Sciences is a blend of theory and research. The program is designed to accredit students in developing analytical skills to identify various management issues and suggest possible solutions through research work. The Master of Philosophy in Management Sciences program builds the capacity of the graduates to pursue PhD in their respective fields of interest. The students are encouraged to explore the deepest, broadest questions of life, why we exist, how society should organize itself, and how institutions should relate to the community.

Purpose

The purpose of human endeavor, to name just a few. The structure of markets, societies and organizations depends on our assumptions about human beings, global resources and human welfare. The program aims at developing a comparative perspective rather than teaching one school of thought. Another important feature of the program is to develop integrated thinking so that students can connect the dots amongst competing political, financial, environmental, ethical, and social interests.

Focus

The program is research-oriented and focuses on academic research with practical applications in business, industry, and real life. The Master of Philosophy in Management Sciences endeavors to produce high-quality professionals who could serve effectively in academia and industry.

Knowledge

The Master of Philosophy in Management Sciences is a Postgraduate research degree program. This wide-ranging, flexible course is primarily designed to provide its graduates with an in-depth knowledge of management practices that are an essential element of professional life in an increasingly complex world of global business.

Duration

Master of Philosophy in Management Sciences  is a full-time Two-year degree that offers several demanding careers in multicultural and international environments in all core areas and disciplines relevant to business management, including high-growth companies, the finance sector, and management consulting firms.
As a specialized research-oriented program, Master of Philosophy in Management Sciences will enable you to develop expertise in solving managerial and technical problems across all areas of business management. It will also help to build the intellectual ability of the graduates for their candidacy in Ph.D. programs.

Develop focus

Our Postgraduate level Management Sciences program is so immersive that it focuses on developing a particular knowledge about management to address complex business problems. You will learn about data management, business intelligence, predictive modeling, and business analytic methods for providing solutions across business functional areas. Its strong emphasis on management and leadership roles, trains enough to lead and manage business change, tackling world-scale problems. 

Learning

Based on solid analytical training, students can learn risk management, critical thinking, and problem-solving by quantitative tools and qualitative methods. It will also refine your soft skills alongside team working, communication, high performance, and decision making.

Course

Our Management Sciences modules contain a wide range of courses in all areas of business and management thatare essential to a successful career on a global scale. The course structure offers a flexible mixture of core/compulsory and optional modules. Through an optional module as credited work placement, you will be provided a chance to gain work experience and develop your professional network. A placement will provide you with valuable experience to add to your CV.

Eligibility Criteria

Department of business education will allow admission with 2.0 CGPA / 2nd division as a basic minimum criterion for admission in MPhil programs.Department will offer five undergraduate business-related basic courses to the students with non-business education.

Tuition Fee

Tuition fees cover the majority of the costs of your study, including teaching and assessment. Fees are charged at the start of each academic semester. If you pay your fees directly to the University, you can also choose to pay in instalments.

Additional course costs

There may be extra costs related to your course for things such as stationery, books, materials, and field trips.

Teaching

The Faculty of Business Education will utilize various teaching methods such as classroom lectures, discussing research articles, hands on trainings, industry visits and field trips, guest speakers, project-based & problem-based learning, brainstorming, simulations, discussions, individual & group assignments, seminars & workshops, role-play, and self-study to educate students. Meanwhile, students can consult their respective tutors if they face problems during consultation hours. Additionally, the university provides them with a wide range of educational resources, both online and on-campus, to support student learning. Recommended textbooks from reputed publishers and pioneer authors in their fields are available in the university’s library to students without an extra charge.

Assessment

All the modules are assessed by coursework, including individual assignments, real-business case studies, interview projects, online quizzes, group work, and poster presentations.
