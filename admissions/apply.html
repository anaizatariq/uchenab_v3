Admissions

Apply

Submit your application through the UChenab online admission portal.

Back to Admissions

Applications to The University of Chenab are submitted through our online admission portal. Registration is free, and you can save your form and return to it before submitting.

Online Admission Portals

Create your account, complete the application form and track your status in one place. Please choose the correct portal based on your degree level.

Apply (Undergraduate) Apply (Postgraduate)

Before you begin

  • A valid, active email address and mobile number.
  • Scanned copies of your Matric / O-Level and Intermediate / A-Level result cards (and degree transcripts for postgraduate applicants).
  • Scanned CNIC or B-Form, plus the CNIC of your father or guardian.
  • A recent passport-size photograph on a plain background.
  • Domicile certificate.
  • Debit card, online banking or cash for the application processing fee.

Applying step by step

Register on the portal

Sign up with your email and mobile number, then verify your account.

Fill in the application form

Complete your personal, academic and contact details carefully - they must match your documents.

Select your programme choices

You may list more than one programme in order of preference.

Upload your documents

Attach clear, legible scans of every required document.

Pay the processing fee

Download the auto-generated challan and deposit it at the designated bank.

Submit and track

Submit the form and use your portal login to track test dates, interviews and merit lists.

After you apply

Shortlisted candidates are called for an entry test and/or interview where the programme requires one. Merit lists are published on the portal and on the university website. Once your name appears on a merit list, deposit your semester dues within the announced deadline to confirm your seat.

Need help with your application?

Email admissions@uchenab.edu.pk or call 053 111 243 622. You can also visit the Office of Admissions at the main campus, G.T. Road, adjacent to Chenab Bridge, Gujrat.
