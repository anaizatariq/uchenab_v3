Our Alumni

The alumni community of The University of Chenab.

Apply Now Explore Programmes

A Lifelong Connection

The University of Chenab Alumni Association is dedicated to fostering a lasting relationship between the university and its graduates. We focus on continuous career guidance, professional counseling, and building a powerful global network of UChenab professionals.

Alumni Scholarships

We believe in nurturing our own. The university proudly offers a dedicated Alumni Scholarship program specifically designed to assist our former undergraduate (BS) students in seamlessly transitioning to and completing their postgraduate (MS/M.Phil) degrees at UChenab.

Endowment Contribution Programme

Scheduled for official launch by December 2026, this visionary program encourages alumni to voluntarily support future scholarships. Participants in this philanthropic initiative will be honored and recognized, potentially through exclusive naming rights for their generous contributions.

Community Engagement

Our relationship does not end at graduation. UChenab regularly invites its alumni back to campus for institutional events, high-level academic seminars, and professional gatherings, fostering a vibrant culture of lifelong learning and active community connection.

Career & Mentorship

The Alumni Association provides continuous career guidance and counseling to help our graduates navigate their professional journeys. Experienced alumni are also encouraged to return as mentors, guiding the next generation of UChenab talent toward industry excellence.

Your place at UChenab starts here

Admissions are open across undergraduate, postgraduate and diploma programmes. Apply online in minutes, or speak with our team for personal guidance.
