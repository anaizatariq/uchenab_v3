Governance

Leadership & Administration

The visionaries stewarding the future of The University of Chenab.

Statutory Body

Board of Governors

The Board of Governors is UChenab's highest statutory body, providing strategic direction, governance oversight, and financial stewardship.

Members are drawn from academia, industry, government and civil society, and meet regularly to review academic quality, appointments, budgets and long-range planning. The 4th Meeting of the Board of Governors was recently held at UChenab, reviewing academic expansion and research strategy.

Composition

  • The Chairman (Chair)
  • The Rector
  • Nominees of the Govt. of Punjab
  • Distinguished Industry Leaders
  • Registrar (Secretary)

Executive Leadership

UChenab is led by a compact executive team that keeps decisions fast, transparent and student-focused.
